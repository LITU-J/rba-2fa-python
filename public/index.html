How It Works

Three-step adaptive authentication

Each login goes through password verification, then an intelligent risk assessment before access is granted.

1

Password Verification

You enter your username and password. The system verifies it against a bcrypt-hashed value — your plain password is never stored.

2

Risk Assessment

The risk engine silently evaluates 7 contextual indicators: device, location, city, login time, frequency, failed attempts, and travel speed.

3

Adaptive Response

Low-risk? You're in immediately. Medium/High risk? OTP challenge. Critical risk? Access is blocked outright.

Risk Engine

Seven contextual indicators

The risk engine scores each indicator using weighted rules (0–100 total) to classify every login attempt.

🌍

IP Geolocation — Country

Login from a country never used before? (+30 pts)

🏙️

IP Geolocation — City

Different city within same country? Catches domestic attackers. (+15 pts)

💻

Device Fingerprint

Unrecognised browser or device for this account? (+25 pts)

🕐

Login Time

Login at a significantly different hour than normal? (+10 pts)

⚡

Login Frequency

Too many attempts in a short window — brute-force signal. (+20 pts)

🚫

Consecutive Failures

Three or more failed attempts in a row — credential stuffing signal. (+25 pts)

✈️

Geo-Velocity

Two distant logins within minutes = physically impossible travel. (+40 pts)

Authentication Tiers

Four adaptive security levels

The total risk score determines what the user must provide in addition to their password.

🟢 Low Risk
0 – 29

Password only. Normal login from a known device and location.

🟡 Medium Risk
30 – 59

Password + TOTP or Email OTP. Something unusual detected.

🟠 High Risk
60 – 84

Password + TOTP or SMS OTP + Email OTP. Multiple suspicious signals detected.

🔴 Critical
85 – 100

Access blocked. Too suspicious to allow under any circumstances.
