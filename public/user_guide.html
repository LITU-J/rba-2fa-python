RBA-2FA System — User Guide

Risk-Based Adaptive Authentication · UNAM Research Prototype 2026

📌 About This System

The RBA-2FA System is a secure login platform that uses Risk-Based Authentication (RBA) combined with Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). Instead of always asking for a second factor, the system analyses your login context and only escalates security when unusual activity is detected.

If you log in normally from your usual device and location, you sign in with just your password. If something looks unusual — a new country, new device, or suspicious IP — the system asks for additional verification.

📝 How to Register

  1. Go to the Register page by clicking "Create one" on the login screen.
  2. Enter a unique username, your email address, and optionally your phone number (needed for SMS verification).
  3. Create a strong password — at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. Example: MyPass@2026
  4. Submit the form. You will be registered and can sign in immediately.
  5. Optionally, set up an Authenticator App (TOTP) from your dashboard for stronger 2FA protection.

🔐 Login Risk Tiers

Every login is scored by the risk engine. Your score determines what verification is required:

🟢 LOW
Score 0–30
Password only. No extra step needed.
🟡 MEDIUM
Score 31–60
Password + one factor (Authenticator app or Email OTP).
🟠 HIGH
Score 61–85
Password + two factors (e.g. Authenticator + Email OTP).
🔴 CRITICAL
Score 86–100
Login blocked. Try from a trusted device or reset your password.

⚠️ What Raises Your Risk Score?

The system checks seven risk indicators during every login attempt:

Indicator Score Added Severity
New Country — logging in from a country never used before +65 High
New Device — browser or device not seen on your account before +30 Medium
New City — logging in from an unfamiliar city within a known country +30 Medium
Consecutive Failures — multiple failed password attempts recently +25 Medium
High Frequency — many login attempts in a short time window +20 Medium
Unusual Time — logging in at an abnormal hour for your account +10 Low
Impossible Travel — location change physically impossible in the time elapsed Blocked Critical

📲 Two-Factor Authentication Methods

  1. Authenticator App (TOTP) — Set up Google Authenticator or Authy from your dashboard. Scan the QR code once, then use the 6-digit rotating code at login. This is the most secure option.
  2. Email OTP — A one-time code is sent to your registered email address. Enter it within the time limit shown on screen.
  3. SMS OTP — If you registered a phone number, a one-time code is sent via SMS. Enter it on the verification screen.
  4. Backup Codes — Generated when you enable TOTP. Store these safely offline. Each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator app.

🔒 Account Lockout

After 5 consecutive failed password attempts, your account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes. The login screen will show a countdown timer. Wait for the timer to finish, then try again with the correct password.

If you believe your account has been compromised, use the Forgot Password link to reset your credentials immediately.

📧 Login Security Alerts

Every time you successfully log in, the system sends a security alert email to your registered address showing your login location, IP address, and time.

If you see a login you do not recognise, click "This wasn't me — Revoke Session" in the email. This immediately logs out all active sessions on your account.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I being asked for a code even though I log in from the same place?
The risk engine may have detected a new device fingerprint — caused by a browser update, cleared cookies, or incognito mode — which raises your risk score and triggers a second factor.
I did not receive my email OTP. What should I do?
Check your spam or junk folder. The code is valid for a limited time — if it expired, return to the login page and start again to receive a fresh code.
My login was blocked (CRITICAL). What does that mean?
The system detected a high-risk pattern such as impossible travel or a very high combined risk score. Try logging in from your usual device and location, or use the Forgot Password flow to regain access.
What is the Risk Engine Demo on the login page?
It is a research simulation tool that lets you test how the system responds to logins from different countries and IP addresses. It is included for demonstration and evaluation purposes only.
Is my data safe?
Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing and are never saved in plain text. Session tokens are unique per login. All login activity is logged for security monitoring only.
